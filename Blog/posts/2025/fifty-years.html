Fifty Years Gone So Soon?

Posted MONTH DAY, 2026

I recently saw that my high school class 50th reunion is coming up this year. How did the years go by so quickly?

My classmates and I have memories of the assassinations of JFK, MLK, and RFK, with Viet Nam, Woodstock, and Watergate on the evening news. We survived polyester bell bottoms, platform shoes and Disco music. Other than that last item, the 1970s had some of the best variety of music - rock and roll, singer songwriters, and more. There were great movies. (Jaws at the drive-in, and many others!)

We spent a few years in a common environment. It was mostly rural, and there were unspoken lessons in hard work and independence. (For me, they included hauling hay, milking a cow, and tending chickens and pigs.) We learned and took it all with us in our lives. Graduating high school was just a beginning.

I am looking forward to attending the reunion, curious to see where life has taken everyone. We all have struggles and triumphs, and no straight roads, just ups and downs with twists and turns. That makes it interesting, and a sense of humor helps, so the stories I hear ought to be amazing.
