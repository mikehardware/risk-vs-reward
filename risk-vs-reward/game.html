RISK vs. REWARD

  • It's not just about knowing the answer—it's about knowing how much to trust yourself.
  • RISK vs. REWARD is a high-stakes trivia game where every answer becomes a decision. After seeing each question and its four possible answers, you'll use the Risk/Reward Slider to decide how much confidence to place behind your choice. Every decision affects both your current bankroll and your path to the $1,000,000 top prize.

    • How the Game Works

    1. Select one of the sets of questions for your game by using the "Select Question Set" pulldown below.
    2. Start by clicking the "Start Playing RISK vs. REWARD" button below to begin.
    3. Select a "Question Category" for your question. The question and possible answers will appear. Read the question and possible answers carefully.
    4. Move the RISK vs. REWARD Slider to decide how much confidence to place behind your answer. As the slider moves, you'll instantly see your potential gain if correct, your potential loss if wrong, and how the decision affects your best- and worst-case paths through the rest of the game.
    5. Use the arrow keys for finer slider control. (Up/Right increases values, Down/Left decreases values.)
    6. Select your answer by clicking on the circle next to the answer you want to choose.
    7. Select the "Lock in Answer" button to reveal the results.
    8. You have 120 seconds to adjust the slider, choose your answer, and lock everything in before time expires. If the timer reaches zero, your current selections are automatically used.
    9. After seeing the results, you can "Go to Next Question" or "Use the Do-Over" (available for the first six questions to try again).
    10. After Question 12, your final total is revealed. Did your strategy pay off—or did caution or overconfidence get in the way? (Sorry, this is just for fun... no real prizes!)
    11. Ready for another round? Click 'Start New Game' to return here and play again!
