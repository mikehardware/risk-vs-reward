RISK vs. REWARD

It's not just about knowing the answer—it's about knowing how much to trust yourself.

RISK vs. REWARD is a high-stakes trivia game where every answer becomes a decision. After seeing a question and its four possible answers, you'll use the Risk/Reward Slider to decide how much confidence to place behind your choice. Every decision affects both your bankroll and your path to the $1,000,000 top prize.

How the Game Works

  1. Select one of the Question Sets below.
  2. Click "Start Playing RISK vs. REWARD" to begin.
  3. Choose a Question Category. A question and four possible answers will appear. Read them carefully.
  4. Move the RISK vs. REWARD Slider to decide how much confidence to place behind your answer.

    As the slider moves, you'll instantly see:
    • Your potential gain if correct
    • Your potential loss if wrong
    • Your highest possible finish
    • Your lowest possible finish
  5. Use the arrow keys for finer slider control. (Up/Right increases values. Down/Left decreases values.)
  6. Select your answer by clicking the circle next to your choice.
  7. Click "Lock in Answer" to reveal the results.
  8. You have 120 seconds to adjust the slider, choose an answer, and lock everything in before time expires. If time reaches zero, your current selections are automatically used.
  9. After seeing the results, choose either "Go to Next Question" or "Use the Do-Over". The Do-Over is available during the first six questions.
  10. After Question 12, your final total is revealed. Did your strategy pay off—or did caution or overconfidence get in the way? (Sorry, this is just for fun... no real prizes!)
  11. Ready for another round? Click "Start New Game" to return here and play again.
